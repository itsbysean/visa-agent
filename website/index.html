GOOD TO KNOW

Which countries and visa types are supported?

The workflow can research multiple countries and purposes. New Zealand is the first country guide; other destinations require official research at runtime. This does not mean every route has been verified in advance.

Can I choose the output folder?

The default root is students/. You can choose another path, such as visas/, and create separate cases for multiple applications by the same person.

Does it submit my application?

The skill prepares and tracks work. It does not submit applications, pay fees, make bookings or contact authorities.

Where is personal information stored?

Case files and backups are stored in the folder you choose. Website demos contain synthetic information only. Information you send to your agent is also subject to that service’s policies; provide only what is needed.
